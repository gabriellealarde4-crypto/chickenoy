Privacy and Policy

Chicken Noy respects customer privacy and uses order information only to process purchases, coordinate delivery, confirm payments, and respond to support requests.

Information We Collect

  • Name, phone number, email address, and delivery address submitted during checkout or contact forms.
  • Order details, cart items, payment method, order status, and customer messages.
  • Optional GCash payment details or proof of payment shared by the customer for verification.

How We Use Information

We use customer information to prepare orders, contact customers about delivery, verify payments, resolve complaints, improve service, and keep accurate order records.

Data Protection

Customer information is handled only by authorized Chicken Noy staff. We do not sell customer data. Information may be shared only when required to complete delivery, comply with law, or protect the business from fraud or abuse.

Customer Requests

For privacy questions or correction requests, contact us at chickenoyofficial@gmail.com or through the Contact Us page.
