About Chicken Noy

Chicken Noy is a local fried chicken shop built around simple, dependable food: crispy outside, juicy inside, served hot for pickup and local delivery.

Our Kitchen Promise

Every order is prepared with quality ingredients, cooked with care, and packed so customers can enjoy the best possible texture and flavor when the food arrives.

What We Serve

We focus on freshly prepared fried chicken, value meals, family bundles, and customer-friendly ordering through our website. Customers can order online, choose Cash on Delivery or GCash, and track their orders from the dashboard.
